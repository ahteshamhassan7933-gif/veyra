FAQ

Do you take a cut of my sales?

No. Ever. Flat monthly SaaS. Your revenue stays 100% yours. We are infrastructure, not an affiliate network.

Where does the money flow?

Straight into your existing payment stack (Stripe, Adyen, Braintree, etc.). Veyra never touches your funds. We only pass signed checkout mandates.

Isn't Shopify's native MCP enough?

MCP alone doesn't get you into ChatGPT Instant Checkout. That needs OpenAI's ACP spec (5 REST endpoints + webhooks + signed feed). We handle both, plus Google AP2, plus AI-visibility tracking.

Are you scraping ChatGPT / Perplexity?

No. We use official APIs and public shopping surfaces where they exist. Our AI-visibility tracker uses documented endpoints only. No ToS violations.

How long does setup take?

Plugin install: 60 seconds. OAuth: 60 seconds. First feed sync: 5 minutes. Live in ChatGPT: 24-72 hours (subject to OpenAI merchant review).

What products can't you support?

We refuse alcohol, gambling, adult content, riba-based financial products, and anything OpenAI/Google restrict. Everything else is welcome.
